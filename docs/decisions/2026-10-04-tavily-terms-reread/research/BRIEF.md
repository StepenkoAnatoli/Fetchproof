## Contradictions and how they were resolved

**One, weighed here for the first time.** The docs FAQ (E-04) lists "SOC 2 certified, zero
data retention" as an advantage of the Search API. The sentence is not new: the 2026-09-28
capture holds it word for word (the two FAQ bodies hash equal) and the requirements note of
that day recorded it as contradicted by the terms. What this reading adds is the weighing.
The Platform Terms (E-01) say in §6.5 that Tavily and its third-party AI providers may "retain
Customer Input submitted to the AI Functionality ... for purposes of training". For a request
that reaches the AI Functionality the two statements cannot both hold; whether an ordinary
search does - the FAQ says LLM-generated answers are optional - is not something these
documents resolve.

**The terms are trusted, for three reasons.** They are the contract and the FAQ is a feature
bullet with no definition of what is retained by whom. The terms say an executed written
agreement that references them, or an Order Form that states its own precedence, supersedes
them, so what could override §6.5 is a document between Tavily and a customer, which a public
page cannot show. And the FAQ's own Support & Privacy section does not repeat the claim: it
links to the privacy policy (E-02), which says query data "may be used to improve our
responses to future queries". A bullet contradicted by both documents it defers to is
recorded, not relied on.

**What the contradiction does and does not change.** It does not meet the trigger: C-6 asks
for a tier under which inputs are not trained on, and a bullet is not a tier. It does give a
future reading a precise question for sales (E-05 is a form, not a page): is "zero data
retention" written into an agreement, for which plan, does it cover §6.5's training clause,
and does it cover ordinary search as well as the AI Functionality? Until a written answer
exists, the public documents say training.

This is the fourth reading in sixteen days (2026-09-19, 09-22 twice, 09-28) and the first to
include the pricing and enterprise pages. The terms and the privacy policy are byte-identical
to the 2026-09-28 captures. The pricing page sells four tiers and names privacy only for
Enterprise, in four words that resolve to a sales form. The trigger - a no-training tier - is
unmet, and the one sentence that sounds like one (E-04, on record since 2026-09-28) is
contradicted by the contract for any request that reaches the AI Functionality.

The fallback the operator asked for is already the kit's: when Firecrawl reports its credits
exhausted, a run stops and says what is left, or with `--fallback` switches to `http-keyless`
or `browser` (ADR-0086, ADR-0129); search has SerpAPI and SearXNG beside Firecrawl's own. A
provider that may train on inputs would not be a fallback for a metered one; it would be a
change in what the research subject is shared for.

Out of scope, explicitly: re-arguing C-6 (the terms have not changed, so the argument has
not); a different keyed search provider, which is its own research project with its own terms
to read; any adapter, flag or configuration key.
