# Movement note: B1, the short-quote floor on the compared text (ADR-0191), 2026-10-25

Command: `node docs/measurements/2026-10-refresh-baseline/movement.mjs` on the working branch
`quotes/quote-level-refresh` with ADR-0191's `quoteTooShort` in the tree (on top of `8c63e77`),
against the baseline measured at `ab2e605`.

## Result

- **The 41 baseline corpora: no movement.** None of the 517 quote anchors changed `tooShort` or
  `found`, no finding moved, no verdict moved.
- **Reported as added, not as movement:** the corpus `docs/decisions/2026-10-25-first-quoted-refresh`
  (committed in `8c63e77`, the first real quoted refresh) - its 4 anchors (E-01..E-04, all found,
  none short) and its 2 refreshes (E-01 -> E-03, E-02 -> E-04). The script prints 7 lines and
  exits 1 because of these additions; none of them comes from ADR-0191.

## Corpora outside the 41-corpus baseline (added after review F2, 2026-10-25)

The note above was measured on top of `8c63e77`, not on B1's own parent `3282cf3`, which added
the punctuation-spacing corpus. The movement script cannot call a corpus outside its baseline
"movement": it lists every anchor of such a corpus as "added". So the three corpora committed
on 2026-10-25 were measured separately.

- **Re-measured on** `build/u1fix`: HEAD `d6b3e5d` plus the unstaged U1-fix change (decision
  5 of ADR-0191: a quote short only on its compared text still warns and is still compared).
- **Method (the review's own):** the full `runPreflight` over every corpus `git ls-files`
  lists (44, without `fetchproof/template`), run three times with `env: {}`. One run uses the
  kit at `3282cf3` (before B1), one the kit at `d6b3e5d` (B1), and one this tree (B1 with the
  fix). The `citations` findings, the verdict and the failing rules are then compared.
  Separately, `quoteTooShort` and `quoteTooShortOnRaw` were compared for each of the 557 quote
  anchors.
- **One anchor moves:** `docs/decisions/2026-10-25-punctuation-spacing`, E-02, the quote
  `[ [edit](/w/index.php?title=Percent_sign&action=edit&section=1) ]`.
  - Before B1, it was checked and found. Citations passed: "9 evidence rows ... 34 quotes found
    in their capture".
  - Under B1 and with the fix, it warns `quote-too-short`: its compared text is `[edit ]`.
    Counts are pass 12 / warn 10 / fail 0, against pass 13 / warn 9 / fail 0 before.
  - The verdict is PASS in all three runs.
  - With the fix the anchor is also still compared (it was long on its raw text) and found,
    so it only warns. Under B1 without the fix it was not compared at all.
- **No other anchor moves**, in either direction. The two measures disagree on no other
  anchor of the 557. No committed quote is short on raw text and long on compared text (the
  NFKC-lengthened class, review F3).
- `node docs/measurements/2026-10-refresh-baseline/movement.mjs` (compare mode) on the same
  tree: no line for any of the 41 baseline corpora. It printed 47 lines, all "added", for the
  three new corpora, and exited 1. Among them, E-02 [5] shows as "added, quote-too-short".
- **Open, for the owner:** whether E-02 fires ADR-0191's reopen trigger: a quote whose
  specificity lives in a link target, in a corpus about markup. E-02 is the evidence BRIEF.md
  reads for the `]` row of ADR-0192's table. The link regex reads `[ [edit](url) ]` as
  `[edit ]`, which is a separate, older defect.

## What this zero does not prove

- Every one of the 517 anchors in the corpora was already long on the compared text, so the cases
  B1 fixes (a link-syntax two-word quote, zero-width padding, formatting-only fragments) have no
  instance in the corpora. They are covered by the unit tests in `fetchproof/test/quotes.test.mjs`,
  not by this measurement.
- It says nothing about rows that no corpus contains, or about a capture that will be taken later.
